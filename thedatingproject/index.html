About the film

The Dating Project is a documentary from Family Theater Productions that follows five single people, ages 18 to 40, as they search for authentic and meaningful relationships in an age of swiping left or right.

Half of America is single. The way people seek and find love has radically changed. The trends of hanging out, hooking up, texting and social media have created a dating deficit. Dating is now . . . outdated.

Questions people ask

Where can I watch The Dating Project?

The full documentary streams free on EWTN+. The link is at familytheater.org/thedatingproject. The film is also on DVD from the HCFM store.

How long is it, and who is it for?

The film runs 69 minutes. It follows five single people between 18 and 40, so it suits young adults, college groups and anyone thinking about how dating has changed.

How do I host a group screening?

Write to license@hcfm.org with your group, the date you have in mind and roughly how many people will come. The team will set up the screening with you.

Is there a discussion guide?

Yes. Curriculum guides are written for five groups: young adults, high school, college, families, and fathers and sons. Leave your email on the curriculum page and the guides download at once.

What is Dating Decoded?

A companion YouTube series made from footage shot during the film's production. It opens up topics the film touches: hookup culture, the effect of media on relationships, and the refusal to commit.
