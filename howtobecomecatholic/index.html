  6. Testimony

    "All of a Sudden I Felt Jesus"

    Testimony7:01

    "I don't want to be Catholic." That's what Kelly Walsh used to tell her husband — 34 years into their marriage, raised by an atheist father, with no belief in sin or in Jesus as the Son of God. The turning point didn't come through an argument, it came through her son. After a life-changing encounter with Christ in college through FOCUS (Fellowship of Catholic University Students), he invited his mom to spend two weeks studying the Bible together. His patience, compassion, and surprising analogy between sin and a pack of gum opened the door to something Kelly never expected: a real encounter with Jesus.

  7. Testimony

    I Was Afraid to Give Up My Heritage

    Testimony6:30

    "I was really a Protestant who was Orthodox and just didn't know it." Confusing as it was, Michael Brazda's conversion story led him to embrace his unique identity in the Catholic Church. Growing up, Michael moved between Protestant and Greek Orthodox churches. After losing his faith in college, he explored Calvinism before moving to Greek Orthodoxy, which led him to reconnect with his Greek heritage. Still feeling unfulfilled, he finally found his way into the fullness of faith in the Catholic Church as an Eastern Rite Catholic.

  8. Testimony

    Married, Expecting, and Newly Catholic

    Testimony8:59

    Bryson grew up evangelical, became a committed atheist and materialist chasing "the evidence," and eventually found his way back to faith through C.S. Lewis's Mere Christianity and G.K. Chesterton. Yeun grew up with a devout Christian mother and an atheist father, drifted through other religions in college, and slowly found herself drawn back in to Christianity. The couple shares their honest, and at times, messy conversion story: the intellectual arguments that convinced them, the OCIA process that gave their faith real structure, the awkwardness of talking about religion with secular family and friends, and what it's actually like to build a new life of prayer, community, and identity as new converts — including navigating convalidation for a Catholic marriage while expecting their first child.

  9. Testimony

    I Left Mormonism and Became Catholic

    Testimony7:24

    After growing up deeply involved in the LDS Church, Alaina never imagined she'd return to organized religion—let alone convert to Catholicism. Negative experiences left her skeptical of religious communities, and after leaving Mormonism she searched everywhere for meaning, exploring New Age spirituality and other paths before encountering Christ in a new way. Alaina talks openly about what it was like comparing the Mormon sacrament to the Catholic Eucharist, why she had to be baptized again in the Trinitarian formula, how prayer became more personal, the hard conversations with her Mormon family about the decision, and about finding a welcoming parish community. Not only was she surprised by the deep meaning in the traditions of the Catholic Church, but her preconceptions of religion were proven wrong—including what it would be like to become friends with a priest.

About the series

How to Become Catholic is a web series from Family Theater Productions, hosted by Katia and Roberto, that answers the most-asked questions about what Catholicism is and how to join the Catholic Church.

We all question the meaning of life. Seek truth. Long for community.

Crave something deeper.

Many turn to religion to satisfy such longing, and many to Catholicism. It is the oldest of all the branches of Christianity and founded by Jesus Christ.

Our new five-episode YouTube series How to Become Catholic explores what Catholicism is and how to join. Our hosts, Katia and Roberto, address the most-asked questions about how to become an active member of the Catholic Church: whether you are not baptized, baptized coming from another Christian denomination, or are returning after an absence.

We will talk about the formation program known as the Order of Christian Initiation of Adults (OCIA), formerly the Rite of Christian Initiation of Adults (RCIA). Hear from personal and expert testimonies on living the Christian life and how the Catholic Church accompanies its faithful after entry.

Dive into the Faith's scriptural and historical roots, uncover the beauty so many find in it, and discover how it creates a home – in Heaven and on earth.
