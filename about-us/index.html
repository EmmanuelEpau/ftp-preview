We make Emmy, Gabriel, and Christopher Award-winning stories that unlock hearts to the possibilities of love, family, and faith.

More about our work

Family and faith-based media is our specialty such as the quirky, fun YouTube series Catholic Central and its Spanish-language compadre Lente Católico. We also produce inspiring documentaries such as PBS's Playing Like a Girl: The House That Rob Built, PBS's Native Ball: Legacy of a Trailblazer, and co-production Broken Mary: The Kevin Matthews Story.

Our latest projects are: documentary The Dating Project, now streaming on EWTN+, preschool series Guillermo & Will, co-produced with Christian platform Minno, and animated series Chime Travelers, which is now available on Formed and Hallow.

Also, we are featured as a resource on the USCCB website. You can find us on their Resources for Youth Ministry Leaders, Resources for Young People, and Resources for Jóvenes pages.

A Creative Environment

Our award-winning team

Our team is composed of top-notch creatives who take heart-tugging storytelling seriously.

Our facilities are on the famed Sunset Boulevard in Hollywood, California, where Family Theater Productions' award-winning media has engaged families since 1947.

Our current productions include feature films, short-format video, video web series, extensive blogging, and social-media content.

Our radio and television shows play daily on stations around the globe.

Updated Studios. Our updated production facilities include a sound studio, production set, screening auditorium, and post-production suites.

We are staffed and equipped for production projects of many proportions.

Our story

From a radio idea in the 1940s to a studio on Sunset Boulevard.

  1. Early 1940s

    Venerable Patrick Peyton realizes he can reach more families across the United States through mass media, namely radio.

  2. 1945

    On Mother's Day, he produces a national radio program with Bing Crosby on the Mutual Broadcasting System, the second-largest radio network at the time. It becomes a series that plays on Mutual for 23 years.

  3. 1947

    He moves to Hollywood and founds Family Theater Productions, producing radio, film and television. He also founds Holy Cross Family Ministries, headquartered in North Easton, Massachusetts.

  4. Today

    Family Theater Productions continues his work as a ministry of Holy Cross Family Ministries.
