About the film

Hour of Our Death is a short documentary from Family Theater Productions, directed by Jonathan Cipiti, about the Little Sisters of the Poor, who give the neediest elderly a home and serve them until their last breath.

Meet the Little Sisters of the Poor. An international congregation of women religious offer a home to the neediest elderly of every race and religion, serving them with grace, love and dignity until they take their last breath.
