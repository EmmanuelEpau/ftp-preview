Episodes

  1. Expectations of Marriage

    Episode 19:29

    There is nothing more exciting than the prospect of true love. We see it in the movies, and hear it in the songs we listen to -- it's impossible to entertain thoughts of romance without certain ideals and expectations! In this episode, five couples sit down to talk about their perceptions of love and marriage before they found "the one."

  2. Falling in Love

    Episode 29:48

    Dating has many different looks in our society. From meet cutes to matchmakers to friends-to-lovers, every couple has a story. In this episode, the couples share their experience of what it was like dating before the "I do."

  3. The Wedding

    Episode 312:13

    The big day is finally here... with all the joys, excitements, and nerve wracking emotions one could feel on their wedding day! The couples walk through what made their big day special, and what it meant for their future to come.

  4. Reality vs. Expectations

    Episode 47:40

    What happens when expectations don't always meet reality after the wedding day? The five couples sit down to talk about the days, months, and years after their wedding, and about how they worked through things when not all went according to plan.

  5. Family Dynamics

    Episode 510:45

    What happens to a marriage when children enter the picture? In this episode of Lasting Marriages, we sit down with the five couples to talk about how family dynamics affected their relationship with each other, and those around them.

  6. Overcoming Hardships

    Episode 612:28

    It's a fact of life that hardship and trial are sure to be present, no matter where you come from or who you are. What happens to married couples when hardship comes their way? Tune in to see how these couples worked through some of their most challenging moments, in this episode of Lasting Marriages.

  7. Rekindling the Spark

    Episode 713:19

    It's no secret that things change after years of being married- physically, emotionally, and spiritually. After years of marriage, with its ups and its downs, what keeps the spark alive? Join us on this week's episode of Lasting Marriages as these five couples talk about what's kept them going for so long.

  8. Love and Faith

    Episode 89:03

    As Catholics, where does faith play a part in marriage? These couples sit down to talk about how their faith has helped and strengthened them over the years.

  9. Lessons Learned, Love Grown

    Episode 97:49

    As this season of Lasting Marriages draws to a close, the couples impart their final pieces of wisdom on what keeps them going, and what they look forward to in the years to come.

About the series

Lasting Marriages: I Do...and I Still Do is a web series from Family Theater Productions that visits long-married couples for advice and encouragement on keeping their vows "until death do us part."

New episodes air on YouTube every Tuesday through the March 19, 2024 finale.

It’s one thing to marry, it’s another to keep the love alive over decades.

Longtime-married couples have much to teach other couples, married or not, but perhaps even more to teach people starting on their marriage journey.

Our YouTube series visits with long-term spouses for advice and encouragement on keeping their vows truly “until death do us part.”

We encourage couples watching this series to look at themselves, in both their joys and sorrows, as working together towards better communication, authenticity, and intimacy.

Let’s learn from the couples in this series how to better love.
