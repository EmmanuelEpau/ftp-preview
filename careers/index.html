Interns: Young Media-Makers

Family Theater Productions staff is proud to support young media-makers through our internship opportunities.

Students from The University of Notre Dame, University of Southern California, Stonehill College, Southern Illinois University, John Paul the Great Catholic University, Cal State Los Angeles, Chapman University, UCLA and many others have interned here.

We offer experiences in script development, digital-media production, film production, social-media, and marketing. Interns must be enrolled in an accredited academic program. Past interns have acquired valuable experience and made great contributions to our productions.

For information on internships, email Information@FamilyTheater.org.
