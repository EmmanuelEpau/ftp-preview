Episodes

  1. Staying Together With the DeRosa Family

    Episode 18:01

    When military deployment to Iraq brings uncertainty, and sacrifice, this family's devotion to prayer becomes their lifeline. Through nightly Rosary prayers online, trusting in God's plan, and holding on to unwavering hope, they navigated the challenges of long-distance, loneliness, and responsibility. This family story offers encouragement for military families, young married couples, and anyone facing trials — demonstrating how perseverance, community support, and especially devotion to God through prayer, can carry you through even the hardest seasons. Witness the joy of homecoming, the beauty of answered prayers, and the blessings that flow from a family life rooted in Christ. From enduring sacrifice to creating a family and building a home together, the DeRosa family story is a moving reminder of God's grace and faithfulness. If you're seeking inspiration, spiritual growth, or practical ways to strengthen your family through prayer, this video will unlock your heart and renew your faith.

  2. Staying Together With the McFaul Family

    Episode 27:00

    Discover the powerful impact of family prayer in this inspiring story about faith, parenting, and trust in God. Follow Andrea and Sean McFaul as they share their journey of rediscovering faith, strengthening their relationship, and raising their children with a Catholic foundation. Learn how Sean came back to the faith in college by way of Andrea's example. From navigating challenges in school to making life-changing decisions, this heartfelt testimony reveals how family prayer has brought them closer together. In this video, you'll see how the McFaul family's commitment to praying together has transformed their family-life, deepened their faith, and provided clarity during discerning moments. Their daughter Clare's experience highlights the benefits of a Catholic education, where the community offered belonging and meaning. See how her parents have modeled strong Christian values by sharing their prayers with their children. If you've ever wondered how to build a Christ-centered home or encourage your children in faith, this story offers practical encouragement and hope.

  3. Praying Through the Storm: A Family's Journey of Loss, Healing, and Hope

    Episode 37:14

    In this powerful story about a Filipino-American family navigating love, loss, and unwavering faith in Los Angeles, Rowena and Dante David share their heartfelt journey. From the devastating pain of miscarriage, unexpected job loss, and a life-changing cancer diagnosis in the family, the couple overcame many hurdles with prayer. Through every trial, their bond is tested, revealing the emotional realities of family life, resilience, and trusting God even in the darkest moments. This inspiring story highlights how prayer, perseverance, and deep-rooted Catholic values helped them overcome hardship and find hope again. From welcoming their son Lorenzo after loss, to witnessing healing and survival of Rowena's Mother, their testimony is a moving reminder that even in uncertainty, faith can provide strength and direction. Their journey reflects the struggles that many families face—grief, financial stress, and fear—while offering encouragement to those seeking purpose and peace through prayer.

  4. From Cancer to Miracles: A Family's Powerful Journey of Faith and Prayer

    Episode 49:01

    The Nicoloro family shares their journey of faith, prayer, and perseverance through life's most unimaginable trials. From a life-threatening cancer diagnosis to a miraculous recovery, this emotional testimony reveals how unwavering trust in God and the power of the Rosary transformed their lives. Learn how this family survived the depths of tragedy with the loss of their child, by facing grief with prayers and faith. Witness how love, sacrifice, and spiritual devotion carried them through fear, uncertainty, and ultimately into healing and hope. In this deeply moving Catholic testimony, Kathleen and Bob Nicoloro share how their marriage began in the shadow of stage IV Hodgkin's lymphoma, and how prayer became their lifeline. After being told they might never have children, their faith was rewarded with a growing family that defied all odds. But their journey also includes profound loss. The heartbreaking passing of their young son Angelo tested their faith in ways they never expected. Through grief, they leaned on prayer, the Rosary, and their belief in eternal life to find strength and healing.

  5. Facing a Rare Genetic Disorder: One Family's Journey of Faith, Love, and Prayer

    Episode 58:01

    The powerful story of the Hernandez family in Los Angeles shares how they navigate faith, love, and resilience after their daughter Claire is diagnosed with an ultra-rare genetic deletion. Doctors warned she may never walk, talk, or speak, leaving her parents overwhelmed and scared. But through prayer, community support, and unwavering trust in God, this family continues to find hope in the middle of hardship. Their emotional testimony is a moving reminder that miracles can appear in unexpected ways, and that we're all blessed with gifts from God. In this inspiring Catholic family's story, hear how faith became the foundation of their marriage, parenting, and daily life. From Xavier's heartfelt relationship with God, his Alpha, to Claire's ability to bring joy and healing to everyone around her with her smile, the Hernandez family shares what it truly means to stay strong through adversity. This touching story explores disability, parenting challenges, spiritual growth, prayer, and the strength of family bonds during some of life's hardest moments, underlining that the family that prays together stays together.

About the series

Staying Together: Families Praying Together is a web series from Family Theater Productions that spotlights Catholic families who overcame obstacles through prayer, built on the idea that "the family that prays together stays together."

Our families can be great sources of happiness but can also face hard challenges and choices surrounding children, work, health, relatives, relocations, or finances.

At times, couples may feel as if they’re bearing their burdens alone, but some spouses hold a special gift that keeps them together.

Staying Together: Families Praying Together is a new YouTube series that spotlights families who, through their deep Catholic faith, overcame obstacles, proving that “the family that prays together stays together.”

You'll hear many powerful testimonies of resilience and grace: from Elizabeth and Vinny DeRosa; Andrea and Sean McFaul; Rowena and Dante David; Kathleen and Bob Nicoloro; and Vivian and John Hernandez.

Discover the determination and resilience of families who overcome all things through the power of prayer.
