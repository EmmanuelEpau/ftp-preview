Episodes

  1. 1

    Entrepreneur & Educator Derry Connolly, Ph.D.

    Episode 18:38

    Derry Connolly, Ph.D., President and co-founder of John Paul the Great Catholic University in Escondido, California, shares his expertise in education, the arts, faith, and entrepreneurship. Moving from his native Ireland eventually to Southern California, the mathematics scholar worked at Kodak and IBM before transitioning into higher ed. As the founder of a Catholic university focused on the creative arts – including filmmaking - - and business innovation, Dr. Connolly functions as leader, teacher, and evangelist.

  2. 2

    Auxiliary Bishop of LA Matthew Elshoff OFM, Cap.

    Episode 27:58

    Matthew Elshoff, Auxiliary Bishop of Los Angeles, shares his wisdom, passion, faith and thoughts on servant leadership. A member of the Capuchin Franciscan order since 1982, Bishop Matthew was appointed Auxiliary Bishop by Pope Francis and ordained in 2023. He currently serves in Our Lady of the Angels province of the Archdiocese of Los Angeles.

  3. 3

    Father Greg Boyle, S.J.

    Episode 39:37

    Father Greg Boyle, S.J., is the director and founder of Homeboy Industries, the world's largest gang intervention and rehabilitation program, located in downtown Los Angeles. In this episode, Father Greg shares his thoughts on leadership, as well as some surprising insights into what he finds important about leading with kinship and compassion. We also hear from "homies" that have been through the program and now work as employees.

  4. 4

    Pierre Landry and Scouting America

    Episode 49:49

    Pierre Landry, the Training Committee Chair of Verdugo Hills Council in Glendale, California, shares his love of all things Scouting America. Discover the power of volunteer and servant leadership in Scouts and how the organization has trained youth and adult leaders to do their best going back to the founding of Boy Scouts by Lord Baden Powell. From family to faith, Pierre reveals the wide variety of skills, activities and events that have made Scouting a tradition that remains relevant to this day.

  5. 5

    Mary Lynne Knighten and Azusa Pacific University

    Episode 57:12

    Mary Lynne Knighten, DNP, RN, NEA-BC is a professor and DNP Program Director at Azusa Pacific University. In this episode Mary Lynne offers encouragement to young nurses and students in healthcare to say "Yes." Sharing her own experiences, faith and even personal trails and triumphs, Mary Lynne's journey as a nurse and leader is both educational and inspirational.

About the series

Effective Leaders: Serving With Purpose is a web series from Family Theater Productions that shares stories from everyday leaders who practice the best practices of leadership in workplaces, churches, schools and civic groups.

Great leadership is at the heart of thriving communities, as ordinary people rise with courage to provide guidance and inspire harmony.

This series shares captivating stories from everyday leaders embodying the best practices of leadership in workplaces, churches, schools, and civic groups.

Get insights from Derry Connolly, Ph.D. (Founding President of John Paul the Great Catholic University); Bishop Matthew G. Elshoff, OFM Cap. (Episcopal Vicar for Our Lady of the Angels Pastoral Region for the Archdiocese of Los Angeles); Rev. Gregory J. Boyle, S.J. (Founder and Director of Homeboy Industries); Pierre Landry (VHC Training Committee Chair and Roundtable Commissioner for Scouting America); and Mary Lynne Knighten, DNP, RN, NEA-BC (DNP Program Director, Associate Professor, and DNP Clinical Residency Coordinator for Azusa Pacific University).

Discover how a commitment to service fuels their strength, and how family, neighbors, and church communities offer unwavering support.

Watch to learn how to lead with impact, empower the next generation, and ignite young hearts to move their communities boldly forward together.
