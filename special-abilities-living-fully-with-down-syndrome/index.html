Episodes

  1. Meet Kristin & Marc

    Episode 16:54

    Meet Kristin Ong, 32, and Marc Williams, 19, two young adults living fully with Down Syndrome. In episode one, we discover Kristin's love of fairy tales, movies, singing, and coloring; and Marc's love of sports (including Special Olympics basketball), dance, pro wrestling, and pasta.

  2. Family

    Episode 26:10

    Family is everything to people with special needs. Kristin shares about her love for her mom and her conflicting feelings about living with Down Syndrome. Also, her cousin/caretaker, Michael, has his own ups and downs providing support for Kristin while living with a disability

  3. Basketball

    Episode 34:53

    Marc and his Mom share about his athletic abilities as a player on the Burbank Special Olympics Basketball Team. With commentary from Coach Douglas Sarine, we learn what makes Marc a great team player, how he is a big fan of the LA Lakers, and how sports can be the perfect "person first" activity for members of the disabilities and Down Syndrome communities.

  4. Memory Lane

    Episode 44:57

    Kristin and her cousin Michael take a stroll down memory lane while looking through old pictures in a photo album. Kristin reminisces about her popular days in high school, and laments about her struggles with college enrollment and entering a day program. She also shares her hopes to go back to school and travel again in the future.

  5. Dancing

    Episode 55:56

    Marc performs with the Free 2 Be Me dancers at the Very Special Arts Festival at the Music Center in downtown LA. Marc's Mom shares about his talent and special abilities as a dancer, from a very young age. With commentary from Dance Instructor Colleen Perry, we learn what makes Marc an exceptional dancer, and why dance is a great activity for people living with disabilities and Down Syndrome.

  6. Fairy Tales and Magic

    Episode 64:04

    Kristin reveals her love of fairy tales and magic by showing us her favorite cosplay costumes and sharing her love of all things Disney. She talks about her dreams of becoming a Hollywood actress and a Disneyland princess. Kristin and her cousin, Michael, visit the Hollywood Walk of Fame and El Capitan Theatre to see "The Little Mermaid."

  7. God & Church

    Episode 76:57

    Kristin and Marc share their faith in God and talk about church. We learn about the ministry that Kristin and Marc have been a part of, Family and Friends of People with Disabilities (FFPD), and we meet ministry leader Emma Guanlao, and Reverend Peter Rogers from St. Dominic church. At Mass, Kristin performs the task of the cantor, leading the church in the responsorial psalm. After Mass, the ministry has a picnic on the patio with food, music, and face painting.

About the series

Special Abilities: Living Fully With Down Syndrome is a Christopher Award-winning web series from Family Theater Productions that shares the stories of two young adults, Kristin Ong and Marc Williams, who live with Down Syndrome.

Millions of people live with Down Syndrome worldwide. This disability affects not only the person but also their family, friends, and community. This Christopher Award-winning series presents the stories of two young adults living with Down Syndrome and the challenges, triumphs, and special abilities they possess.

Through interviews, we will learn from their perspective, and hear about their feelings, concerns, and dreams. By sharing these experiences, in their own voice, we will show the dignity and beauty of their lives, as well as the care shown by their supporters and loved ones.

Special Abilities focuses on Kristin Ong, 32, and Marc Williams, 19, highlighting their talents and skills, and showing them participating and excelling at their favorite activities and hobbies.

People living with Down Syndrome have been given rare and special gifts, sometimes beyond our acknowledgment or comprehension. This series shows how a so-called “disability” can become a blessing, and reveals how love and faith become keys to living life fully.
