Grace in Grief: Hope After Loss: trailer

  • Series
  • 7 episodes

Grace in Grief: Hope After Loss is a web series from Family Theater Productions that explores how people survive the pain of loss, through interviews with the brokenhearted and the healed.

Grief is all-consuming. Grief is all-too-common. Sometimes, it seems like grief is all there is. The earthly world is truly a vale of tears. Every day, there’s some tragic news: every day, someone loses a spouse, a child, or the will to live itself. Every day, many of us think: “That will never happen to me.” Until it does. And then what? How, as human beings, can we find the strength to carry on? To love and lose, again and again and again? “What’s the point?” we cry in our despair. But that’s exactly it: the point of being human is to strive in love. Day after day, we must strive, even as the whole world crashes down in darkness around us. That’s the bad news. But the good news is: grief is not all there is. There’s also God’s grace to sustain us and keep us striving. We weep and we pray and—we survive. In Grace in Grief: Hope After Loss, the new video series by Family Theater Productions, survival of loss is exactly what we’ll explore. Through interviews with the brokenhearted and healed, those weeping and those praying, we will raise awareness about the process of grief. Our guests will share stories about how they’ve survived the pain of loss, and how they honor their beloved departed. Join us as we dive into the darkness of grief, seeking to bring suffering souls one step closer to light. Get our series study guide here.
