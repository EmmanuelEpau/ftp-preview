Creating Sacred Beauty is a web series from Family Theater Productions in which Katia Villanueva explores what draws artists in many media to sacred art and how their faith informs their creativity.

Creating Sacred Beauty is an interactive journey of discovering not only the artwork, but the ingenuity and talent behind it. It features artists creating in a variety of media: Sister Rose Marie Tulacz, SND; Sister Lupe Hernandez, FSP; Sisters Emmanuel Kwak and Tiziana Del Masetto; Rev. Fidelis Omeaku; Michael Spooner; Jason Leith; Lalo Garcia; Lisa Hendey; David Judson; Jefferson Tortorelli; Ximena Palencia; Bryan Roach; Wassim and Anton Razzouk; Silvana and Wayne Johnson; Sequoia Sierra; and Christy Hernandez. Connecting the artistic process to the unique human hearts of these individuals is the emotional core of this series. Katia Villanueva uncovers a deeper understanding of artisans devoted to creating sacred beauty to explore what draws these artists to use their gifts and how their faith informs their creativity. We all, as humans, uniquely yearn for something beautiful. No matter our upbringing, creed, religion, or personal taste, art lifts our minds to the divine. By art's power, we are stirred and become more connected. Art, especially religious art, is integral to cultures all over the world...but what is special about particularly sacred art, or art oriented towards the act of worship? When people have found purpose in their craft as Christians, it inspires us also to reflect on the gifts we have and the meaning we can and do add to the world.
