The Carpenter: trailer

  • Series

The Carpenter is a planned Bible-based series from Family Theater Productions that will explore the life of Joseph as a father and husband in first century Israel.

Joseph has inspired parents throughout history, through story, painting, sculpture, and stained glass. A new series is coming that will explore his life through the lens of a father and husband, as he recognizes the awesome responsibilities entrusted to him. It’s a heroic story of his tireless effort to defend, lead, and provide for his wife and son amid the struggles and realities of daily life in first century Israel. Rooted in biblical moments and history, each episode offers an authentic journey into the hidden years of the greatest love story never told. The ultimate role model of love, unity and strength found in family, where life begins, faith is born, and love never ends. Heroic stories that uplift and celebrate the family are essential. Fewer are choosing marriage and children. Strong models of faith, parenthood and especially fatherhood are needed. The Carpenter, narrative, Bible-based series, will do just that. Bible-based content is part of the history of Family Theater Productions, and in recent years, has proven itself a family audience favorite. While it is a bullseye for a faith-based audience, these types of shows and others have proven popular with mainstream viewers. Bible-based content interest has soared with series such as The Chosen (2019-2024, Angel Studios, 2024-today, Lionsgate) and The Bible (2013, History Channel). “The Chosen” Season 1 - highest crowdfunded feature project of all time with more than 200 million viewers. “The Bible” - one of the highest TV ratings with 13.1 million viewers· Faith-based films have increased through theatrical releases and streaming. “Cabrini” (2024), “Jesus Revolution” (2023), “Mother Teresa: No Greater Love (2022)”, “Fatima”( 2020). Family Theater Productions' team of producers and writers, with Church and Bible experts, is creating The Carpenter, a television series bringing the most renowned family in history alive on the screen. It will not happen without people like you who understand the importance of family, faith and the power of storytelling to unlock hearts to God.
